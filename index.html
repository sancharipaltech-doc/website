About me

Dr. Sanchari Pal is a renowned gynaecologist with particular focus on high risk obstetrics, complex infertility, and advanced gynaecological endoscopy. She has the experience of delivering over 2000 plus happy lives and helping thousands of women. She is particularly focussed on helping women right from birth through old age with a sharp focus on adolescent gynecology, menopause endocrinology and preventive oncology.

She completed her post graduate studies from the prestigious Seth GS Medical College and KEM Hospital, Mumbai and undergraduate from the renowned Nil Ratan Sircar Medical College and Hospital, Kolkata. She holds a gold medal in obstetrics and gynaecology and is the recipient of multiple nationally recognised awards including the DK Tank Prize and the Chuni Lal Mukherjee Award. In 2014, she was awarded ISSN Best Publication of the Year.

She is the managing committee member of the coveted Mumbai Obstetric and Gynecological Society, the Indian Menopause Society among others and has active academic contributions throughout the country. Apart from being a qualified gynecologist, she is a strong proponent of women's rights and believes in compassionate women's healthcare across all ages, accessible to all strata of women.

How can I help?

Pregnancy & Childbirth

Care through your pregnancy and delivery, vaginal birthing including water birthing and C-sections, instrumental delivery including forceps and vacuum assisted, high risk pregnancy, fetal Doppler scans and epidural pain relief.

Fertility & IVF

Fertility check-ups and natural conception, IUI, IVF/ICSI treatment, including egg freezing, ovarian tissue cortex cryopreservation, embryo freezing, egg retrieval, ICSI, frozen embryo transfer, genetic testing of embryos (PGT) and semen preparation. Legions of experience with regenerative therapies like PRP and stem cells.

Keyhole gynae surgery

Advanced Laparoscopy and hysteroscopy for fibroids (myomectomy), ovarian cysts, endometriosis, fertility enhancing surgeries, uterine polyps, septum and adhesions, smaller cuts and faster recovery.

Periods & women's health

Irregular or painful periods, endometriosis, adenomyosis, adolescent gynaecology, cervical cancer vaccination, and safe, confidential MTP.
